The following flow shows how the WFS_CMD_API_SECURE_COMMAND and
WFS_INF_API_SECURE_QUERY commands are called to wrap the WFS_CMD_CDM_DISPENSE and
WFS_INF_CDM_PRESENT_STATUS commands. The WFS_CMD_CDM_PRESENT command is used to
present the bills to the user. This might be the case where the device has an intermediate stacker and requires a
specific present command:
